# V0.6 Full HQ denoise design

The denoiser is causal and one-pass: it needs only the current source frame, the previous denoised history and the current->previous motion field. No future-frame lookahead or intermediate image sequence is required.

For each pixel:

1. Read the current 3x3 neighborhood and convert samples to YCoCg.
2. Calculate local min/max, luma variance and an edge/detail measure.
3. Validate the current->previous flow against a second previous->current DIS solve. Photometric residual and forward/backward consistency are multiplied into the confidence mask, which rejects many occlusions and bad vectors before denoising.
4. Use the validated V0.5 motion vector to sample the previous denoised history at the corresponding previous-frame position.
5. Clip that history sample into an expanded current-neighborhood YCoCg box. The expansion grows with local variance so random noise is not immediately rejected.
6. Calculate temporal acceptance from optical-flow confidence, luma disagreement, chroma disagreement, motion magnitude, history age and local edge strength.
7. Blend accepted history with the current frame. Stable pixels progressively gain history age and therefore stronger denoising.
8. In Full HQ mode, use an edge-aware 3x3 spatial filter as a fallback for new/disoccluded/low-confidence pixels. The spatial pass is reduced on strong edges and when good temporal history is already available.
9. On a scene cut, history is discarded and rebuilt from the new shot.

The raw decoded frame is always used for optical-flow estimation. Denoised output is never recursively fed back into the optical-flow input, preventing the motion estimator from drifting toward its own filtered history.

The denoised frame is then used as DLSSNR Color and as the input to Depth Anything V2. This is deliberate: both the neural-rendering color input and the inferred depth guidance become more temporally stable.
